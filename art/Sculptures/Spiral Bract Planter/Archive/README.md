# Spiral Bract Planter - archive

Earlier iterations, each with its own `.scad`, `.3mf`, render and leaf workshop script, as built.

| Iteration | What it was |
|---|---|
| [iteration_1](iteration_1/) | Solid, dome-shaped bracts on a golden-angle spiral, 56 leaves (seed 7). 197 x 195 x 202 mm, 0 % past 60 deg, 880 g / 16 h 13 min on the H2C |

The current design (iteration 2, in the folder above) went back to iteration 1's solid leaves after
a thin-blade attempt: blades cut through each other, and the corrections for that made the slicer
report floating regions. It adds per-leaf bends, a 2.5 mm edge lip, a drain and a matching saucer.
